## Brand & Style
This design system defines a luxury sports-performance editorial experience. The identity sits at the intersection of haute horlogerie precision engineering and elite athletic performance. It evokes quiet power, aerodynamic perfection, tactile obsidian gloss, and clinical craftsmanship reminiscent of top-tier experimental skunkworks.

The visual style blends **Minimalism** with **Dark Modern Skeuomorphic Lighting and Ambient Refraction**. Surfaces are built from infinite deep gloss blacks, brushed titanium accents, and ultra-fine hairline borders, punctuated by chromatic iridescence—a subtle, ethereal ambient bloom of sunset rose, sky blue, and twilight lavender derived from specular reflections on lacquered carbon fiber.

## Colors
The palette is rooted in an abyss of high-gloss and matte carbon neutrals, illuminated by pure titanium tones and soft chromatic ambient light.

- **Primary (`#F4F4F6`):** Pure Titanium White. Used for hero titles, decisive calls-to-action, and primary brand indicators.
- **Secondary (`#A1A1AA`):** Anodized Platinum. Used for technical specifications, subheadings, and secondary actions.
- **Tertiary (`#C4B5FD`):** Prismatic Lavender. Reserved for high-value highlights, active states, and specular reflection accents alongside delicate rose (`#FDA4AF`) and sky (`#93C5FD`) iridescent tints.
- **Neutral Surface (`#0F0F11`):** Deep Obsidian. The foundation canvas, providing maximum depth and contrast for lacquer finish highlights.
- **Elevated Surfaces (`#18181B`, `#27272A`):** Precision matte composite and dark anodized aluminum container tiers.
- **Structural Borders (`#27272A` & `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`):** Hairline edges capturing directional light.

## Typography
The typography marries mechanical aerospace authority with refined contemporary digital fashion.

- **Headlines & Technical Metrics:** Set in `Space Grotesk`. Geometric, unapologetically athletic, with distinctive angular cuts that echo precision machining and bespoke carbon decals.
- **Editorial & Body Text:** Set in `Manrope`. Clean, legible, with balanced proportions and warm geometric undertones that prevent clinical fatigue during immersive long-form storytelling.
- **Labels & Micro-Tags:** Set in tracked uppercase `Space Grotesk` (`label-caps`), functioning like laser-etched serial numbers and industrial certification markers.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a disciplined 12-column editorial grid engineered for asymmetric negative space and hero-driven presentation.

- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12 columns, fixed maximum width of `1440px`, centered with `4rem` outer margins and `2.5rem` gutters. Large expansive full-bleed zones allow floating 3D bat hardware to break boundaries.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns, fluid with `2rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Stacks side-by-side spec callouts into dynamic 4-column pairing grids.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, fluid with `1.25rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters. Editorial hero modules collapse to edge-to-edge interactive carousels with pinned sticky glass purchasing bars.
- **Spatial Rhythm:** Built on an 8pt architectural step. Micro padding (`space-2xs` to `space-sm`) governs internal componentry, while macroscopic rhythm (`space-2xl` to `space-4xl`) spaces out narrative chapters and engineering breakdowns.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation mimics the deep gloss surface reflections of the lacquered bat against soft studio backlight, relying on delicate ambient glows rather than blunt muddy shadows.

- **Level 0 (Baseplate):** Pure `#0F0F11`, non-reflective canvas.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Layer):** `#18181B` with a subtle 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)` and a non-directional floor occlusion shadow: `0px 8px 24px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Showcase):** `#202024` with top-edge specular highlight `inset 0px 1px 0px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` and an ambient iridescence underglow: `0px 20px 40px -15px rgba(196, 181, 253, 0.08), 0px 10px 20px -10px rgba(253, 164, 175, 0.05)`.
- **Level 3 (Overlays, Drawers & Menus):** High-density smoked glass: `rgba(15, 15, 17, 0.82)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, enclosed in a dual border consisting of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` and a soft shadow `0 24px 60px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`.

## Shapes
Geometry is refined, confident, and aerodynamic. The baseline roundedness is anchored at `0.5rem` for micro interactive components, while larger structural cards and media viewports scale gracefully up to `rounded-2xl` (1.25rem / 20px) to mirror the sleek, fluid curve of high-end composite sporting equipment. Full pill geometries (`rounded-full`) are reserved exclusively for technical badges, luxury micro-chips, and circular action triggers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Titanium Sheen):** Background is `#F4F4F6` with text in `#0F0F11`. Font is `Space Grotesk` Medium, uppercase micro-spaced (`label-caps`). Roundedness is `rounded-full` or `rounded-xl` (height `48px`). On hover: transform lift of `-1px` with specular white ambient glow (`0 0 20px rgba(244, 244, 246, 0.3)`).
- **Secondary Action (Dark Obsidian):** Smoked background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text `#F4F4F6`. On hover: surface brightens to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)` with border `#C4B5FD`.
- **Ghost / Text Action:** No fill, titanium text with an animated 1px underline that expands from center with iridescent lavender gradient.

### Badges & Chips
- **Engineering / Tech Badges:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), height `24px`, padding `0 10px`. Background is `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` with a hairline border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`. Typography is `label-caps`. Optional status dot glowing in iridescent lavender (`#C4B5FD`) or titanium green (`#34D399`).
- **Selection Chips (Weight / Drop Specs):** Squircle `rounded-xl`, height `40px`, padding `0 16px`. Inactive: `#18181B` with muted text `#71717A`. Selected: `#27272A` with `#F4F4F6` text and an internal highlight ring of `1px solid #F4F4F6`.

### Cards & Feature Containers
- **Product & Feature Showcases:** Background `#18181B` with `rounded-2xl` (20px radius). Hairline border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Content features deep imagery paired with high-contrast `Space Grotesk` typography and floating translucent specs. Hover introduces an ultra-subtle pastel gradient wash across the top border.

### Input Fields
- **Search & Spec Inquiries:** Height `48px`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`, `rounded-xl`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)`. Text is `#F4F4F6` with placeholder in `#52525B`. Focus state initiates a titanium border transition to `#F4F4F6` and a diffuse ambient shadow.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Custom Selectors:** Dimensions `20px x 20px`, `rounded-md` (checkbox) or `rounded-full` (radio). Border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`. In active state, filled with `#F4F4F6` and an inset `#0F0F11` check or dot mark.

### Specialized Components
- **Spec Telemetry Strip:** A horizontal data HUD presenting metrics (Exit Velo Rating, Barrel Taper, Drop Ratio) featuring `spec-num` values paired with stacked `label-caps` metadata tags.
- **Sticky Glass Commerce Deck:** Pinned mobile viewport bar utilizing smoked backdrop blur, price display, and an instantaneous "Acquire" trigger button.
